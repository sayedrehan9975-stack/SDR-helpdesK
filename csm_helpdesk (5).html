🔍 FAQs – 1st Year Students

Minimum attendance? ▶
75% in all subjects. 65–75%: condonation allowed. Below 65%: debarred.
How to check internal marks? ▶
Go to Marks & Results. UT marks appear within 5 days. Re-evaluation: contact teacher within 3 days.
Last date to pay fees? ▶
April 30, 2026. ₹500 late fee per week. Scholarship adjusted automatically.
How to get bonafide certificate? ▶
Apply at Academic Section (Room 104) or raise a ticket. Issued in 2 working days with college ID.
How is SGPA calculated? ▶
SGPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits) ÷ Total Credits. O=10, A+=9, A=8, B+=7, B=6, C=5.
How to access college Wi-Fi? ▶
Connect to "CSM-StudentNet". Use college email + student ID. Issues? Raise IT ticket.
How to apply for scholarship? ▶
Submit Form SM-01 + mark sheets to Scholarship Office. Deadline: May 20, 2026. Need 75%+ attendance & 7.5+ SGPA.
